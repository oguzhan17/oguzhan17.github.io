Paylaşım Merkezi

Paylaşım Merkezi is a personal scheduling panel that its owner runs on his own computer to publish his own videos to his own Instagram account (How Things Began) and YouTube channel (How Things Began). It is not a public service: nobody else can sign in to it or use it.

It uses the official Instagram Graph API and the YouTube Data API. Read the privacy policy to see what it stores and where.
