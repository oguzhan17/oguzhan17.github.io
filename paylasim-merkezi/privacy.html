Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Paylaşım Merkezi is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who runs it on his own computer to schedule and publish his own videos to his own Instagram account and YouTube channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uses the Instagram Graph API (Meta) and the YouTube Data API (Google) to upload the owner's videos, set their titles, descriptions, covers and publishing times, read back their status, and change or delete uploads the owner cancels.

Where data is kept

Everything stays on the owner's computer: the videos, covers and captions he prepares, the posting schedule, and the login tokens (in the macOS Keychain). Nothing is sent to any server of this tool, because it has none. Data goes only to Meta and Google, through their official APIs, to publish the owner's own posts.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can disconnect an account in the panel's settings at any time, which deletes its tokens from the computer, and can revoke access in his Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in his Facebook settings under Business Integrations. Uploaded videos are managed in Instagram and YouTube Studio.

Google and YouTube

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
